# Extra features you can add

These are ranked by how much they help an SDS, formulation and SOP business. Most only need a well-known plugin; no custom code.

## High value – do first

| Feature | Why | How |
|---|---|---|
| Card / PayPal / Apple Pay payments | Global buyers expect them | *WooCommerce Stripe Gateway*, *WooCommerce PayPal Payments* |
| Multi-currency (USD, EUR, GBP, AED, INR…) | Worldwide clients see local prices | *CURCY* or *WOOCS* |
| Reliable email | Order and download emails must arrive | *WP Mail SMTP* + Brevo, Mailgun or Google Workspace |
| SEO | Rank for "SDS for Amazon", "ISO 17025 SOP" and similar searches | *Rank Math* or *Yoast*; write blog posts and FAQ pages |
| Security & backups | You store client formulas | *Wordfence*, *UpdraftPlus* (daily off-site backup), strong passwords + 2FA |
| Speed / caching | Better sales and SEO | Host caching or *LiteSpeed Cache*, plus Cloudflare (free) |
| WhatsApp chat button | Many B2B clients prefer it | *Click to Chat* / *Joinchat* |
| GDPR cookie banner | Needed for EU/UK visitors | *Complianz* |

## Grow the business

- **Free SDS sample / lead magnet.** Offer a free download such as "Amazon hazmat checklist" in exchange for an email address (*MailPoet* or *Mailchimp for WooCommerce*).
- **Document previews.** Watermarked first-page PDF previews on formulation and SOP products build buyer trust.
- **Bundles.** For example, "17025 Starter Pack" = kit + uncertainty SOP + validation SOP at a discount (*WooCommerce Product Bundles*).
- **Subscriptions.** Offer "SDS maintenance": yearly updates when regulations change (*WooCommerce Subscriptions*).
- **Membership / document library.** Charge one fee for access to all SOPs (*Paid Memberships Pro* or *WooCommerce Memberships*).
- **Coupons and volume discounts.** Already built in; for tiered SDS pricing such as 5+ products, use *Discount Rules for WooCommerce*.
- **Reviews and testimonials** with Amazon-seller success stories.
- **Multilingual site** (Arabic, Spanish, French, Chinese) with *TranslatePress* or *WPML*.
- **Online consultation booking** for 17025 gap analysis or formulation calls (*Amelia* or *Bookly*).
- **B2B invoices / quotes as PDF** (*PDF Invoices & Packing Slips for WooCommerce*).
- **Affiliate programme** for consultants who refer clients (*AffiliateWP*).
- **Tax / VAT for digital goods** sold to EU/UK buyers: *WooCommerce Tax* or *Quaderno*.

## Possible custom developments (built on Bionexa Core)

- Customer portal where clients see SDS status (Received → Classifying → Draft → Delivered) and download finished SDS from *My Account*.
- Instant SDS price calculator: region × languages × number of products.
- Per-region pricing on the SDS form, for example +$X for each extra country.
- Automatic SDS-revision reminders emailed to clients after 3 years or after a regulation change.
- Simple GHS classification pre-check from an uploaded composition, as a lead generator.

## Important notes

- **Legal pages.** The Terms, Licence and Refund pages are templates. Have a lawyer review them for your country.
- **SDS liability.** Keep the disclaimer that SDS are based on customer-supplied data. Consider professional indemnity insurance.
- **Downloads.** Keep *Downloads → File download method* set to *Force downloads* (already set) so file links can't be shared publicly.
